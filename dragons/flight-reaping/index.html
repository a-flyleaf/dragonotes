The Lost Souls of Flight Reaping

Not comprehensive, but the ones I remember most distinctly.

or: That one time I let the Hunger Games simulator dictate a lair purge, stashed records in a dragon bio, then got sick of the bio graphics breaking & re-ported everything over to my own webbed site

Apocrypha & Lux

Best friends and former members of Viral Evolution, if only on the outskirts. Apocrypha was a devoted warrior; Lux, a would-be Light Flight Representative who left home too soon. When VE dissovled, the duo pledged their allegiances to the wandering empress Vulcan; it was Apocrypha’s idea.

Alas, rather than reclaimed glory, the would-be empire dissolved into darkness, taking these two with. Since then they’ve lingered in the Tangled Wood as helpful voices, urging fellow lost souls not to follow their paths.

Vulcan

A fallen empress, once the ruler of a mighty empire in the Ashfall Waste. But when it was conquered by a rivaling kingdom, Vulcan was exiled. She returned ages later to a vacant territory, every trace of former glory gone. Infuriated, Vulcan set out to recruit warriors to rebuild her throne, promising high ranks and splendor to those who joined.

Unfortunately, recruitment was a slow endeavor. The longer it dragged on, the more existing recruits questioned Vulcan’s plans and motives. Unwilling to admit defeat to the end, Vulcan instead led her would-be army deep into the Tangled Wood, where they (to the surprise of no one) met a grisly end among the thorns.

Wore the accent Blazing Plumage.

Tyson

A lonesome boy who, throughout his short life, was haunted by Vulcan personally. Little is known of his story beyond this, but he was killed by Delphi and Vulcan has been awfully quiet since. Tyson’s voice is barely more than a whisper; Mallow has been trying to coax it out more.

First egg I ever hatched. Never quite got the lore Spark (har har) so, uh, yeah.

Apidae

A reckless little lizard who really liked bumbles. Former traveling partner of Fern, but when Apidae got lost in the brambles, she forgot about his existence entirely… or so it once seemed.

For ages Apidae was restless, tying travelers’ paths in knots just to feel something. But Apidae has been soothed by the knowledge Fern is still alive and well, and (with some prompting) remembers him after all. It’s almost enough to put his soul at peace for good. (Almost.)
